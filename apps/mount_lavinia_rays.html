Bathymetry: The west coast shelf off Mount Lavinia is Sri Lanka's narrowest (<10 km), reaching ~22 m depth at 3 km offshore. The headland models the rocky promontory on which the Mount Lavinia Hotel sits. Physics: Rays follow Hamilton's equations for linear water waves with the full tanh dispersion relation. Time integration uses leapfrog (2nd-order) after a forward Euler first step. Wave climate: SW monsoon swell from ~225° compass at 30° incidence from the shore normal (dominant May–September). Rays bending toward the headland show wave energy focussing — the physical reason surf is stronger at the hotel end of the beach.
