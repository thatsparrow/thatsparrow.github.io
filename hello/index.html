FOR DEVELOPERS:

I’m a front-end–leaning hybrid developer with a background in data science.

Over the past 10+ years, I’ve mostly worked independently, though I’ve also led a team of four during a period of rapid scaling.

Beyond the Web, I’ve built applications for Android, GNU/Linux, Windows, Arduino, and even POS terminals. I used to chase every new framework, tool, and trend, but over time I’ve narrowed my stack and become much more intentional about what I focus on.

And, perhaps most notably, I haven’t really written my own code since 2025. With AI and coding agents becoming part of my workflow, my role has shifted from writing code line by line to thinking about problems, designing solutions, directing agents, and reviewing what they produce. Tech moves fast, but these days, I’m less interested in keeping up with everything and more interested in using the right tools to build the right things.
